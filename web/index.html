Sources & method

BC Ferries’ quarterly operations reports give actual round trips and passenger counts. Vehicle capacity uses automobile equivalents (AEQs), a standard amount of vehicle deck space. Passenger-only services can have empty vehicle measures. Each quarterly row links to its source tables.
